Backyard Dominoes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Backyard Dominoes is a dominoes game made by Brian Hennington. This page explains what information the game uses. The short version: we don't collect anything about you.

What the game keeps

The game saves your progress on your own device: your coins, gems, level, the backyard you've built, your settings, and the avatar and name you make for yourself. This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the game, it is deleted too.

What we don't collect

  • We don't ask you to make an account.
  • We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • The game has no ads and no ad tracking.
  • The game has no analytics and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • The game does not use your camera or microphone.

Purchases

You can buy gems and coins in the game. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only that a purchase went through, so the game can add what you bought. Apple's own privacy policy covers how Apple handles purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting information (for example, if online play is added), we will update this page before that happens and change the date at the top.
