Common questions

I bought gems or coins but didn't get them. Close the game and open it again. If they're still missing, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, then email us.

I got a new phone. Where did my progress go? Progress is saved on your device only, so it doesn't move to a new phone. The Starter Pack can be restored on a new phone with Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles all payments. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.
